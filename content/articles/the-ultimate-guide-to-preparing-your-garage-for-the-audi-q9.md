## Step-by-Step Guide
### Introduction to Audi Q9 Maintenance
The Audi Q9 requires regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance and longevity. In this section, we'll guide you through the key maintenance tasks to keep your Q9 in top condition.

#### Checking and Replacing the Engine Oil
1. Locate the oil dipstick under the hood and pull it out
2. Wipe the dipstick clean with a lint-free cloth
3. Insert the dipstick back into the oil reservoir and pull it out again
4. Check the oil level and color, looking for any signs of contamination or degradation
5. If the oil level is low, add the recommended type and amount of oil

#### Replacing the Brake Pads
1. Jack up the vehicle and remove the wheels
2. Locate the brake caliper and remove the caliper bolts
3. Pull the caliper away from the rotor and remove the old brake pads
4. Install the new brake pads and reassemble the caliper
5. Tighten the caliper bolts to the specified torque (35 Nm)

#### Rotating the Tires
1. Check the owner's manual for the recommended tire rotation pattern
2. Loosen the lug nuts on the first wheel using a lug wrench
3. Raise the vehicle and remove the wheel
4. Rotate the tire to the next position and hand tighten the lug nuts
5. Lower the vehicle and tighten the lug nuts to the specified torque (120 Nm)

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always refer to your owner's manual for specific instructions and guidelines
* Use the correct type and amount of oil for your vehicle
* Avoid over-tightening brake caliper bolts, as this can cause damage to the brake system
* Rotate your tires regularly to ensure even tread wear and extend the life of your tires
