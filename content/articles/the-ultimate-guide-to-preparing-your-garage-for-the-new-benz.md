The first step in preparing your garage for the new Mercedes-Benz GLA is to assess your garage's electrical capacity. This involves checking your electrical panel to ensure that it can handle the increased power demands of the vehicle's charging system.

#### Step 1.1: Check Your Electrical Panel
Check your electrical panel to determine its current capacity and whether it can handle the additional load of the Level 2 charger.

#### Step 1.2: Determine Your Charging Needs
Determine your charging needs based on your vehicle's specifications and your personal driving habits.

#### Step 1.3: Upgrade Your Electrical Panel (if necessary)
If your electrical panel is not capable of handling the increased power demands, you may need to upgrade it. This involves installing a new electrical panel with a higher capacity and ensuring that it is properly connected to your garage's electrical system.

The next step is to install a Level 2 charger, which is capable of charging your vehicle at a rate of up to 240 volts. This involves installing a dedicated 240-volt circuit and ensuring that it is properly connected to your electrical panel.
