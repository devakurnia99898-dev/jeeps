## Technical Deep Dive
The 2026 Jeep Wrangler Whitecap is powered by a 3.6L V6 engine that produces 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. The engine is paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and a two-speed transfer case. The Whitecap also features a heavy-duty suspension system with gas-filled shock absorbers and a stabilizer bar. The electrical system includes a 160-amp alternator and a 700-amp maintenance-free battery.

Common failure points on the Whitecap include the plastic impeller in the water pump, the serpentine belt, and the front axle shafts. It's also important to keep an eye on the oil and coolant levels, as well as the condition of the air filter and spark plugs.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Oil Change
#### Step 1.1: Gather materials
You'll need a socket wrench, a drain pan, and enough oil to fill your engine. The recommended oil for the 2026 Jeep Wrangler Whitecap is 5W-20.
#### Step 1.2: Warm up the engine
Drive your Whitecap for a few minutes to warm up the engine. This will help the oil drain more easily.
#### Step 1.3: Find a safe place to drain the oil
Make sure the surface is level and apply the parking brake. Locate the oil drain plug and position the drain pan underneath.
#### Step 1.4: Remove the oil drain plug
Use a socket wrench to remove the oil drain plug. Be prepared for the oil to start draining out.
#### Step 1.5: Replace the oil filter
Once the oil has stopped draining, replace the oil filter with a new one. Hand tighten the filter in a clockwise direction.

### Step 2: Tire Rotation
#### Step 2.1: Gather materials
You'll need a lug wrench and a tire pressure gauge.
#### Step 2.2: Loosen the lug nuts
Before you jack up the vehicle, loosen the lug nuts on the first wheel using the lug wrench. Turn them counterclockwise until they're loose, but don't remove them yet.
#### Step 2.3: Jack up the vehicle
Use a jack to lift the vehicle until the wheel is off the ground. Make sure the jack is securely in place and the vehicle is stable.
#### Step 2.4: Remove the lug nuts and wheel
Completely remove the lug nuts and flat washers, then pull the wheel straight off the hub. Set the wheel aside and move on to the next one.

### Step 3: Suspension Inspection
#### Step 3.1: Gather materials
You'll need a flashlight, a socket wrench, and a torque wrench.
#### Step 3.2: Inspect the shocks
Look for any signs of leaks or damage on the shocks. Check the bushings and mounts for wear.
#### Step 3.3: Inspect the springs
Check the springs for any signs of sagging or damage. Make sure they're properly seated and aligned.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always refer to your owner's manual for specific instructions on how to perform maintenance tasks on your Whitecap.
* Don't over-tighten the lug nuts - this can damage the wheel or brake rotor.
* Make sure to check the tire pressure regularly, as under-inflated tires can lead to poor handling and reduced fuel efficiency.
