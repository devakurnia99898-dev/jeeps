## Technical Deep Dive
The Shelby Super Snake's powertrain is its strongest asset, with a potent V8 engine producing over 700 horsepower. However, this power must be harnessed and translated into traction, which is where the suspension and drivetrain come into play. The Super Snake's independent suspension is well-suited for on-road handling, but it may not provide the necessary articulation and travel for extreme off-road terrain. Common failure points include:
- The stock shocks, which may not be able to handle the stresses of off-road driving
- The axles, which may be prone to breakage under heavy loads
- The engine's cooling system, which may be insufficient for extreme temperatures

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Suspension Upgrade
The suspension upgrade is a critical component of preparing your Shelby Super Snake for off-road dominance. This involves replacing the stock shocks with heavy-duty, off-road-specific units.
#### Step 1: Remove the Stock Shocks
Remove the stock shocks and inspect the mounting points for any damage or corrosion.
#### Step 2: Install the New Shocks
Install the new shocks, ensuring proper torque specs (35 ft-lbs for the upper mount, 45 ft-lbs for the lower mount).
#### Step 3: Adjust the Suspension
Adjust the suspension to the desired height and stiffness, taking into account the vehicle's weight distribution and intended use.

### Axle Upgrade
The axle upgrade is a complex process that requires specialized tools and expertise. This involves replacing the stock axles with heavy-duty, off-road-specific units.
#### Step 1: Remove the Stock Axles
Remove the stock axles and inspect the mounting points for any damage or corrosion.
#### Step 2: Install the New Axles
Install the new axles, ensuring proper torque specs (50 ft-lbs for the axle nuts, 70 ft-lbs for the hub nuts).
#### Step 3: Weld the Axle Housings
Weld the axle housings to the frame, ensuring a strong and durable connection.

### Engine Cooling Upgrade
The engine cooling upgrade is a critical component of preparing your Shelby Super Snake for off-road dominance. This involves replacing the stock cooling system with a heavy-duty, off-road-specific unit.
#### Step 1: Remove the Stock Cooling System
Remove the stock cooling system and inspect the mounting points for any damage or corrosion.
#### Step 2: Install the New Cooling System
Install the new cooling system, ensuring proper torque specs (20 ft-lbs for the radiator mounts, 30 ft-lbs for the hose fittings).
#### Step 3: Test the Cooling System
Test the cooling system, ensuring proper function and leak-free operation.
