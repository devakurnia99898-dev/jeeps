## Step-by-Step Guide
In this section, we'll provide a detailed, step-by-step guide on how to perform common maintenance tasks on the Audi Q9.

### Oil Change
The oil change is one of the most critical maintenance tasks for the Audi Q9. Here's how to do it:
#### Draining the Oil
Locate the oil drain plug and position a drip pan underneath. Use a 10mm socket to loosen the plug, then allow the oil to drain completely.
#### Replacing the Oil Filter
Use a filter wrench to loosen and remove the oil filter. Dispose of the old filter responsibly and install a new one.
#### Refilling the Oil
Use a funnel to pour in the new oil, taking care not to spill any. Replace the oil cap and start the engine to circulate the new oil.

### Brake Pad Replacement
Replacing the brake pads on the Audi Q9 requires some technical expertise, but it's a task that can be completed with the right tools and knowledge. Here's how:
#### Removing the Wheels
Use a lug wrench to loosen the wheels, then raise the vehicle on a lift or jack stands.
#### Removing the Caliper
Use a 12mm socket to loosen the caliper bolts, then slide the caliper off the rotor.
#### Installing the New Brake Pads
Use a pair of pliers to compress the brake piston, then install the new brake pads.
