## **Step-by-Step Guide**
### **Oil Change**
#### Step 1: Warm Up the Engine
Drive the vehicle for a few minutes to warm up the engine. This will help the oil drain more easily.
#### Step 2: Locate the Oil Drain Plug
Find the oil drain plug, usually located at the bottom of the engine.
#### Step 3: Remove the Oil Drain Plug
Use a 10mm socket to remove the oil drain plug. Be prepared for the oil to start draining out.
#### Step 4: Replace the Oil Filter
Remove the old oil filter and replace it with a new one. Hand tighten the filter in a clockwise direction.
### **Brake Pad Replacement**
#### Step 1: Remove the Wheels
Remove the wheels to access the brake calipers.
#### Step 2: Remove the Caliper
Use a torque wrench to loosen the caliper bolts. Remove the caliper and set it aside.
#### Step 3: Remove the Old Brake Pads
Pull out the old brake pads and discard them.
#### Step 4: Install the New Brake Pads
Insert the new brake pads and ensure they are properly seated.
### **Spark Plug Replacement**
#### Step 1: Locate the Spark Plugs
Find the spark plugs, usually located on the side of the engine.
#### Step 2: Remove the Spark Plug Wire
Pull off the spark plug wire to access the spark plug.
#### Step 3: Remove the Spark Plug
Use a spark plug socket to remove the spark plug. Be careful not to round the spark plug hole.
#### Step 4: Install the New Spark Plug
Insert the new spark plug and hand tighten it in a clockwise direction.

## **Pro Tips & Common Mistakes**
* Always use the correct type of oil for your vehicle, as specified in the owner's manual.
* Never over-tighten the oil drain plug, as this can damage the threads.
* Use a torque wrench to ensure the correct torque specs are met when tightening bolts and nuts.
* Don't forget to reset the maintenance light after completing a service task.

1. **Q: What type of oil should I use in my Mercedes-Maybach S 680?**

2. **Q: How often should I change my brake pads?**
A: Brake pads should be changed every 30,000 to 50,000 miles, depending on driving conditions.
3. **Q: Can I use a regular spark plug in my Mercedes-Maybach S 680?**
A: No, the Mercedes-Maybach S 680 requires a high-performance spark plug, specifically designed for its engine.
4. **Q: How do I know if my vehicle needs a tune-up?**
A: If your vehicle is experiencing decreased performance, rough idling, or decreased fuel efficiency, it may need a tune-up.
