## Technical Deep Dive
The Ferrari F40's engine is a 2.9L twin-turbo V8, producing 478 horsepower. The engine features a dry-sump lubrication system, which requires regular oil changes and filter replacements. The transmission is a 5-speed manual, with a limited-slip differential.

Common failure points include the turbochargers, which can fail due to excessive heat and wear. The engine's timing belt should be replaced every 30,000 miles to prevent engine damage.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Oil Change
#### Step 1.1: Locate the Oil Drain Plug
Find the oil drain plug located at the bottom of the engine. Use a 10mm socket to remove the plug.

#### Step 1.2: Drain the Oil
Allow the oil to drain into a pan. Replace the oil drain plug and tighten to 30 ft-lbs.

#### Step 1.3: Replace the Oil Filter
Use an oil filter wrench to remove the oil filter. Install a new oil filter and hand tighten.

### Step 2: Filter Replacement
#### Step 2.1: Locate the Air Filter
Find the air filter located in the engine compartment. Remove the air filter housing and pull out the old filter.

#### Step 2.2: Install the New Filter
Install a new air filter and replace the air filter housing. Ensure the housing is securely fastened.

### Step 3: Timing Belt Replacement
#### Step 3.1: Remove the Timing Belt Cover
Use a 12mm socket to remove the timing belt cover. Mark the timing belt alignment with a marker.

#### Step 3.2: Remove the Timing Belt
Use a torque wrench to loosen the timing belt tensioner. Remove the timing belt and replace with a new one.

#### Step 3.3: Replace the Timing Belt Tensioner
Install a new timing belt tensioner and tighten to 50 ft-lbs.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the correct type of oil and filter for your Ferrari F40.
* Don't over-tighten the oil drain plug, as this can damage the threads.
* Use a torque wrench to ensure accurate tightening of bolts and nuts.

## Cost Analysis: DIY vs Mechanic
The cost of maintaining your Ferrari F40 can vary depending on the task and whether you choose to DIY or hire a mechanic. A typical oil change can cost between $100-$300, while a timing belt replacement can cost between $1,000-$2,000.
