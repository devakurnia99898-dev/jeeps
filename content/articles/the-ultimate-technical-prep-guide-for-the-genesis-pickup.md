## Step-by-Step Guide
### Initial Inspection
#### Checking the Oil and Fluids
1. Locate the oil dipstick and pull it out to check the oil level.
2. Check the color and consistency of the oil to ensure it's not contaminated.
3. Repeat this process for the transmission, coolant, and brake fluids.

#### Inspecting the Tires
1. Check the tire pressure using a tire pressure gauge.
2. Inspect the tread depth and look for any signs of uneven wear.
3. Rotate the tires according to the manufacturer's recommendations.

### Suspension and Steering
#### Inspecting the Shocks and Struts
1. Jack up the vehicle and support it with jack stands.
2. Inspect the shocks and struts for any signs of leakage or damage.
3. Check the bushings and mounts for any wear or damage.

#### Checking the Steering System
1. Inspect the power steering pump and reservoir for any signs of leakage.
2. Check the steering gear and linkage for any play or damage.
3. Test the steering system by turning the wheel and checking for any unusual noises or vibrations.

### Brake System
#### Inspecting the Brake Pads
1. Jack up the vehicle and support it with jack stands.
2. Remove the wheel and inspect the brake pads for wear.
3. Check the brake rotors for any signs of warping or damage.

#### Checking the Brake Fluid
1. Locate the brake fluid reservoir and check the fluid level.
2. Check the color and consistency of the brake fluid to ensure it's not contaminated.
