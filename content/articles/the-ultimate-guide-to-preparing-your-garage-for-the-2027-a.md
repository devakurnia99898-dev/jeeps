## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Prepare the Garage
#### Step 1.1: Clear the Workspace
Clear the garage floor of any clutter or obstacles to create a safe working area.
#### Step 1.2: Install a Lift or Jack Stands
Install a lift or jack stands to elevate the vehicle, providing easy access to the underside.
#### Step 1.3: Set Up a Toolbox
Organize your tools and equipment in a designated toolbox, making it easy to find what you need.

### Step 2: Acquire Necessary Tools and Equipment
#### Step 2.1: Purchase a Torque Wrench
Invest in a high-quality torque wrench to ensure accurate tightening of bolts and nuts.
#### Step 2.2: Invest in an Air Compressor
An air compressor will come in handy for various tasks, such as inflating tires and powering pneumatic tools.
#### Step 2.3: Get a Set of Jack Stands
Jack stands will provide additional support and safety when working under the vehicle.

### Step 3: Familiarize Yourself with the Vehicle's Technical Specifications
#### Step 3.1: Read the Owner's Manual
Thoroughly read the owner's manual to understand the vehicle's features, maintenance requirements, and technical specifications.
#### Step 3.2: Research Online Resources
Utilize online forums, repair manuals, and technical guides to gain a deeper understanding of the vehicle's systems and components.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always refer to the owner's manual for specific instructions and guidelines.
* Avoid using generic or low-quality tools, as they may damage the vehicle or compromise safety.
* Keep the garage well-organized and tidy to prevent accidents and lost tools.

The cost of preparing your garage for the 2027 Dodge Durango will depend on the tools and equipment you already have. If you're starting from scratch, the initial investment may seem significant. However, by doing the work yourself, you'll save money in the long run and develop a deeper understanding of your vehicle.

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. **What is the Redeye paint option, and how do I maintain it?**
The Redeye paint option is a unique finish previously exclusive to the Charger. To maintain its vibrant color, avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. Instead, use a mild soap and water, and dry the vehicle with a soft cloth.
2. **What tools do I need to purchase for my garage?**
The necessary tools will depend on the specific tasks you plan to perform. However, a basic set should include a torque wrench, air compressor, jack stands, and a set of sockets.
3. **How often should I inspect my garage and tools?**
Regularly inspect your garage and tools to ensure they are in good working condition. Check for signs of wear, damage, or corrosion, and replace or repair items as needed.
4. **Can I perform maintenance tasks on my 2027 Dodge Durango myself?**
Yes, many maintenance tasks can be performed yourself, such as oil changes, tire rotations, and brake pad replacements. However, more complex tasks may require professional assistance.
5. **What safety precautions should I take when working in my garage?**
Always wear safety glasses and gloves, and ensure the garage is well-ventilated. Avoid wearing loose clothing, and keep long hair tied back to prevent it from getting caught in machinery.
