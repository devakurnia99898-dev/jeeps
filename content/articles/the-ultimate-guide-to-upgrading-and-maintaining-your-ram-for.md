The engine and transmission can also be upgraded with performance parts such as cold air intakes, exhaust systems, and transmission coolers. Common failure points include the plastic impeller in the water pump, the belts and hoses, and the brake pads and rotors.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Upgrading the Suspension
The suspension system is critical to the Ram 1500's off-road and racing performance. Upgrading to a coilover suspension system can improve handling and stability.
#### Installing Coilovers
1. Raise the vehicle on a lift and remove the wheels.
2. Remove the old shocks and springs.
3. Install the new coilovers, making sure to tighten the bolts securely.
4. Adjust the coilovers to the desired height and stiffness.

### Upgrading the Engine and Transmission
Upgrading the engine and transmission can improve the Ram 1500's power and performance.
#### Installing a Cold Air Intake
1. Remove the old air intake system.
2. Install the new cold air intake, making sure to secure it to the engine compartment.
3. Connect the new air intake to the engine.

#### Installing an Exhaust System
1. Remove the old exhaust system.
2. Install the new exhaust system, making sure to secure it to the vehicle's frame.
3. Connect the new exhaust system to the engine.

#### Installing a Transmission Cooler
1. Remove the old transmission cooler.
2. Install the new transmission cooler, making sure to secure it to the vehicle's frame.
3. Connect the new transmission cooler to the transmission.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Don't over-tighten the bolts when installing new suspension components, as this can damage the threads.
* Make sure to properly align the new exhaust system to avoid any leaks or damage.
* Don't forget to check the transmission fluid level after installing a new transmission cooler.
