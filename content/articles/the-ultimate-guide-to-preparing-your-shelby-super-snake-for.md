## Technical Deep Dive
The Shelby Super Snake's 5.0L supercharged V8 engine is a marvel of modern engineering, producing an astonishing 825 horsepower. However, this power output comes at a cost, as the engine's cooling system must be capable of dissipating massive amounts of heat. The Super Snake's cooling system features a high-performance radiator, heat exchangers, and a sophisticated cooling fan system.

The vehicle's suspension is equally impressive, featuring a fully independent suspension with coilovers and stabilizer bars. However, the Super Snake's suspension is designed primarily for on-road performance, and modifications may be necessary to optimize it for off-road use.

Common failure points to watch out for include the engine's cooling system, particularly the radiator and heat exchangers, which can be prone to damage from debris and overheating. The suspension's coilovers and stabilizer bars may also require upgrading or revalving to handle the stresses of off-road driving.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Suspension Upgrades
The Super Snake's suspension is designed primarily for on-road performance, and modifications may be necessary to optimize it for off-road use. This can include upgrading to heavier-duty coilovers, revalving the shocks, and installing stabilizer bars.

#### Step 1.1: Remove the Wheels and Suspension Components
Remove the wheels, coilovers, and stabilizer bars to access the suspension components. Use a 10mm socket to remove the coilover bolts and a 12mm socket to remove the stabilizer bar bolts.

#### Step 1.2: Inspect and Clean the Suspension Components
Inspect the suspension components for damage or wear and clean them thoroughly. Use a wire brush to remove any debris or corrosion from the coilover and stabilizer bar mounts.

#### Step 1.3: Install the Upgraded Coilovers and Stabilizer Bars
Install the upgraded coilovers and stabilizer bars, ensuring proper torque specs are followed. Use a torque wrench to tighten the coilover bolts to 40 ft-lbs and the stabilizer bar bolts to 30 ft-lbs.

### Step 2: Cooling System Upgrades
The Super Snake's cooling system is designed to handle the massive heat output of the engine, but upgrades may be necessary to ensure optimal performance in off-road conditions.

#### Step 2.1: Remove the Radiator and Heat Exchangers
Remove the radiator and heat exchangers to access the cooling system components. Use a 10mm socket to remove the radiator bolts and a 12mm socket to remove the heat exchanger bolts.

#### Step 2.2: Inspect and Clean the Cooling System Components

#### Step 2.3: Install the Upgraded Cooling System Components

### Step 3: Brake Upgrades

#### Step 3.3: Install the Upgraded Brake Components
