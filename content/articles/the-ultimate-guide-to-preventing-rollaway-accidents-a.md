## Symptoms or Signs to look for
If your vehicle is experiencing any of the following symptoms, it may be at risk of a rollaway accident:
* The vehicle rolls away when parked on an incline
* The parking brake is not engaging properly
* The door-lock microswitch is corroded or damaged
* The vehicle's transmission is not shifting into park properly

## Technical Deep Dive
The door-lock microswitch is a critical component in the vehicle's locking system. It is responsible for sending an electrical signal to the vehicle's computer to engage or disengage the locks. When the microswitch becomes corroded, it can cause the locks to malfunction, leading to a rollaway risk. The microswitch is typically located on the driver's side door and is connected to the vehicle's wiring harness.

Common failure points in the door-lock microswitch include:
* Corrosion due to exposure to water or humidity
* Wear and tear on the microswitch's electrical contacts
* Damage to the microswitch's housing or mounting bracket

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Locate the Door-Lock Microswitch
The door-lock microswitch is typically located on the driver's side door. You will need to remove the door panel to access the microswitch.

#### Step 1.1: Remove the Door Panel
Use a screwdriver to remove the screws that hold the door panel in place. Gently pull the panel away from the door to access the microswitch.

#### Step 1.2: Disconnect the Wiring Harness
Use a wrench to loosen the bolt that holds the wiring harness in place. Carefully pull the harness away from the microswitch.

### Step 2: Inspect the Microswitch
Use a wire brush to clean any corrosion or debris from the microswitch. Inspect the microswitch for any signs of damage or wear.

#### Step 2.1: Check for Corrosion
Use a multimeter to check for any signs of corrosion on the microswitch's electrical contacts. If corrosion is present, use an electrical contact cleaner to clean the contacts.

#### Step 2.2: Check for Damage
Inspect the microswitch's housing and mounting bracket for any signs of damage. If damage is present, replace the microswitch with a new one.

### Step 3: Replace the Microswitch (if necessary)
If the microswitch is damaged or corroded, replace it with a new one. Make sure to follow the manufacturer's instructions for installation.

#### Step 3.1: Connect the Wiring Harness
Use a wrench to tighten the bolt that holds the wiring harness in place. Make sure the harness is securely connected to the microswitch.

#### Step 3.2: Reinstall the Door Panel
Use a screwdriver to reinstall the door panel. Make sure the panel is securely fastened to the door.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Use a wire brush to clean any corrosion or debris from the microswitch
* Make sure to follow the manufacturer's instructions for installation
* Do not use excessive force when tightening the bolt that holds the wiring harness in place
* Do not touch any electrical components with your bare hands
