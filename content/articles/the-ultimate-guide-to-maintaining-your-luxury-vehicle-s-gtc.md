## Technical Deep Dive
The Continental GTC engine is a 6.0L W12 engine that produces 626 horsepower. It features a twin-turbocharged system, which provides increased power and efficiency. The engine also features a dry sump lubrication system, which helps to reduce oil consumption and increase engine longevity. Common failure points in the Continental GTC engine include the turbochargers, fuel injectors, and spark plugs.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Prepare the Vehicle
#### Step 1.1: Locate the Engine
The Continental GTC engine is located at the front of the vehicle. Make sure to familiarize yourself with the engine compartment before starting work.
#### Step 1.2: Disconnect the Battery
Disconnect the battery to prevent any accidental starts or electrical shocks.
### Step 2: Check the Oil and Fluids
#### Step 2.1: Check the Oil Level
Use a dipstick to check the oil level. Make sure it is at the recommended level.
#### Step 2.2: Check the Coolant Level
Use a coolant level sensor to check the coolant level. Make sure it is at the recommended level.
### Step 3: Inspect the Belts and Hoses
#### Step 3.1: Inspect the Serpentine Belt
Check the serpentine belt for any signs of wear or damage. Replace it if necessary.
#### Step 3.2: Inspect the Coolant Hoses
Check the coolant hoses for any signs of wear or damage. Replace them if necessary.
### Step 4: Replace the Spark Plugs
#### Step 4.1: Locate the Spark Plugs
The spark plugs are located on the side of the engine. Make sure to consult the repair manual for the correct location.
#### Step 4.2: Remove the Spark Plug Wire
Use a spark plug wire puller to remove the spark plug wire.
#### Step 4.3: Remove the Spark Plug
Use a spark plug socket to remove the spark plug.
#### Step 4.4: Install the New Spark Plug
Install the new spark plug and tighten it to the recommended torque specification (15 ft-lbs).
### Step 5: Replace the Fuel Injectors
#### Step 5.1: Locate the Fuel Injectors
The fuel injectors are located on the intake manifold. Make sure to consult the repair manual for the correct location.
#### Step 5.2: Remove the Fuel Injector
Use a fuel injector puller to remove the fuel injector.
#### Step 5.3: Install the New Fuel Injector
Install the new fuel injector and tighten it to the recommended torque specification (10 ft-lbs).

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the correct tools and follow the recommended torque specifications to avoid damaging the engine.
* Make sure to consult the repair manual before starting any work on the Continental GTC engine.
* Common mistakes include over-tightening the spark plugs or fuel injectors, which can cause damage to the engine.
