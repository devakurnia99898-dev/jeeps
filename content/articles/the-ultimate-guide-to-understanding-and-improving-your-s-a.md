## The Diagnostic / The Situation
The J.D. Power U.S. Automotive Performance, Execution, and Layout (APEAL) Study is an annual evaluation that focuses on how owners feel about their new vehicles after the first 90 days of ownership. Rather than simply counting mechanical problems, J.D. Power evaluates 37 attributes across 10 vehicle categories. In the context of Jeep owners, understanding and improving APEAL can significantly enhance the overall driving experience. Symptoms or signs to look for include decreased performance, inefficient execution of vehicle functions, and layout issues that hinder accessibility and usability.

## Technical Deep Dive
The APEAL study assesses various aspects of a vehicle, including its performance, features, and overall design. For Jeep owners, this means examining the vehicle's engine, transmission, suspension, and electrical systems. Common failure points or areas for improvement may include the engine's air filter, spark plugs, or fuel injectors, as well as the suspension's shocks, struts, or coil springs. Additionally, electrical system issues can arise from faulty sensors, wiring, or connectors. Understanding how these systems work and identifying potential weaknesses can help Jeep owners address APEAL-related concerns.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Perform Routine Maintenance
#### Check and replace the air filter
Check the air filter for dirt, dust, or debris. If it's dirty, replace it with a new one. This can improve engine performance and fuel efficiency.
#### Change the oil and filter
Change the oil and filter according to the recommended schedule. This can help maintain engine performance and prolong its lifespan.
#### Inspect and replace the spark plugs
Inspect the spark plugs for wear or damage. If necessary, replace them with new ones. This can improve engine performance and fuel efficiency.

### Step 2: Inspect and Improve the Suspension
#### Check the shocks and struts
Check the shocks and struts for signs of wear or damage. If necessary, replace them with new ones. This can improve ride quality and handling.
#### Inspect the coil springs
Inspect the coil springs for signs of wear or damage. If necessary, replace them with new ones. This can improve ride quality and handling.
#### Check the suspension bushings
Check the suspension bushings for signs of wear or damage. If necessary, replace them with new ones. This can improve ride quality and handling.

### Step 3: Troubleshoot Electrical System Issues
#### Check the battery and charging system
Check the battery and charging system for signs of wear or damage. If necessary, replace the battery or repair the charging system. This can improve electrical system performance and reliability.
#### Inspect the wiring and connectors
Inspect the wiring and connectors for signs of wear or damage. If necessary, repair or replace them. This can improve electrical system performance and reliability.
#### Use a scan tool to diagnose issues
Use a scan tool to diagnose any issues with the electrical system. This can help identify problems and guide repairs.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Regular maintenance is key to improving APEAL. Neglecting routine maintenance can lead to decreased performance and increased repair costs.
* Don't overlook the importance of proper tire inflation and alignment. This can improve ride quality, handling, and fuel efficiency.
* Be cautious when working with electrical systems. Incorrect repairs or modifications can lead to safety issues and damage to the vehicle.
