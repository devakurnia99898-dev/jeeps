## Step-by-Step Guide
### Understanding the New Lighting System
The new lighting system introduced by Acura is a complex network of LEDs, control units, and wiring. To understand how it works, one must first identify the components involved and their roles. This includes the LED headlights, daytime running lights, and the sophisticated control unit that manages the lighting patterns and intensity.

#### Identifying Components
- **LED Headlights:** These are the primary light sources and are usually located in the front of the vehicle.
- **Daytime Running Lights (DRLs):** These are designed to increase visibility during the day and are integrated into the front bumper or headlight assembly.
- **Control Unit:** This is the brain of the operation, controlling when and how the lights turn on, including the adaptive lighting features.

#### Troubleshooting Common Issues
Common issues with the lighting system can range from faulty LEDs to malfunctioning control units. Troubleshooting involves using a diagnostic scanner to read any error codes stored in the vehicle's computer, followed by a visual inspection and possibly a voltage test using a multimeter.

### Replacing LED Components
If a fault is found in an LED component, replacement may be necessary. This involves careful disassembly of the headlight or DRL assembly, removal of the faulty LED, and installation of a new one. It's crucial to ensure the new LED is compatible with the vehicle's system and to follow proper installation procedures to avoid damaging the surrounding electronics.

#### Disassembling the Headlight Assembly
- Remove the screws holding the headlight assembly in place.
- Gently pull the assembly away from the vehicle, taking care not to damage any wiring or clips.
- Use a Torx screwdriver to remove any additional fasteners holding the LED module in place.

#### Installing the New LED
- Align the new LED module with the mounting points.
- Secure the module using the Torx screws, ensuring they are tightened to the specified torque.
- Reassemble the headlight assembly in the reverse order of disassembly.
