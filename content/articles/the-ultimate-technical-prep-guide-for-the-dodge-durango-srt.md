## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Oil Change
#### Step 1.1: Locate the Oil Drain Plug
Locate the oil drain plug, which is usually located at the bottom of the engine, near the front of the car.
#### Step 1.2: Jack Up the Vehicle
Jack up the vehicle using a hydraulic jack, ensuring it is stable and secure.
#### Step 1.3: Remove the Oil Drain Plug
Remove the oil drain plug using a 10mm socket, and allow the oil to drain into a pan.
#### Step 1.4: Replace the Oil Filter
Replace the oil filter using an oil filter wrench, and hand tighten the new filter.
### Step 2: Transmission Service
#### Step 2.1: Locate the Transmission Pan
Locate the transmission pan, which is usually located at the bottom of the transmission, near the front of the car.
#### Step 2.2: Remove the Transmission Pan Bolts
Remove the transmission pan bolts using a 12mm socket, and allow the transmission fluid to drain into a pan.
#### Step 2.3: Replace the Transmission Filter
Replace the transmission filter, and hand tighten the new filter.
### Step 3: Supercharger Maintenance
#### Step 3.1: Locate the Supercharger
Locate the supercharger, which is usually located on top of the engine, near the front of the car.
#### Step 3.2: Remove the Supercharger Belt
Remove the supercharger belt, using a torque wrench to loosen the belt tensioner.
#### Step 3.3: Inspect the Supercharger
Inspect the supercharger for any signs of wear or damage, and replace it if necessary.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the correct type and amount of oil and transmission fluid, as specified in the owner's manual.
* Avoid over-tightening the oil drain plug or transmission pan bolts, as this can damage the threads.
* Regularly inspect the supercharger and transmission for any signs of wear or damage, and replace them if necessary.
