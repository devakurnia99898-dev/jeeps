## Technical Deep Dive
The 6.7L Cummins turbo diesel engine is a high-performance powerplant, producing significant torque and horsepower. The engine features a cast-iron block, aluminum cylinder head, and a variable geometry turbocharger. The engine's fuel system includes a high-pressure common rail fuel injection system, which provides precise control over fuel delivery. Common failure points in this engine include the fuel injectors, turbocharger, and engine bearings. The transmission and drivetrain are also critical components, with the truck featuring a heavy-duty automatic transmission and a four-wheel-drive system with a two-speed transfer case.

Use a filter wrench to remove the oil filter. Dispose of the old filter responsibly and install a new one.

### Fuel Filter Replacement

#### Replacing the Fuel Filter
