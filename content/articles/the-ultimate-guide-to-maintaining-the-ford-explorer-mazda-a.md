## Step-by-Step Guide
### Oil Change
#### Draining the Oil
1. Locate the oil drain plug underneath the vehicle.
2. Use a socket wrench to loosen the plug.
3. Allow the oil to drain into a pan.
#### Replacing the Oil Filter
1. Locate the oil filter on the top of the engine.
2. Use a filter wrench to loosen and remove the filter.
3. Install a new oil filter and hand tighten.

### Brake Pad Replacement
#### Removing the Wheels
1. Loosen the lug nuts using a lug wrench.
2. Raise the vehicle using a jack.
3. Remove the lug nuts and wheels.
#### Replacing the Brake Pads
1. Locate the brake caliper and remove the caliper bolts.
2. Use a C-clamp to compress the caliper piston.
3. Remove the old brake pads and install new ones.

### Timing Chain Replacement
#### Removing the Timing Cover
1. Locate the timing cover on the front of the engine.
2. Use a socket wrench to remove the cover bolts.
3. Remove the timing cover.
#### Replacing the Timing Chain
1. Locate the timing chain and sprockets.
2. Use a chain breaker to remove the old chain.
3. Install a new timing chain and sprockets.
