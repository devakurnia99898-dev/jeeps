## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation and Inspection
#### Checking the Oil and Fluids
1. Locate the oil dipstick under the hood and pull it out to check the oil level.
2. Check the oil color and consistency to ensure it's not dirty or contaminated.
3. Check the coolant, brake, and transmission fluid levels to ensure they're at the recommended levels.

#### Inspecting the Tires
1. Check the tire pressure using a tire pressure gauge to ensure it's at the recommended level.
2. Inspect the tread depth and look for any signs of uneven wear or damage.
3. Check the tire valves and ensure they're not damaged or leaking.

#### Inspecting the Suspension and Steering
1. Jack up the vehicle and inspect the suspension components, including the coil springs, control arms, and track bar.
2. Check for any signs of wear or damage on the suspension components.
3. Inspect the steering system, including the steering gear and tie rods, to ensure they're in good condition.

### Maintenance and Repair
#### Oil Change
1. Locate the oil drain plug under the vehicle and remove it using a 10mm socket.
2. Drain the oil into a pan and replace the oil filter.
3. Refill the oil with the recommended type and amount.

#### Brake Pad Replacement
1. Jack up the vehicle and remove the wheels to access the brake calipers.
2. Remove the caliper bolts using a 12mm socket and slide the caliper off the rotor.
3. Remove the old brake pads and install the new ones, ensuring they're properly seated and secured.

#### Suspension Inspection and Repair
1. Inspect the suspension components, including the coil springs, control arms, and track bar, to ensure they're in good condition.
2. Check for any signs of wear or damage on the suspension components and replace them as needed.
3. Tighten any loose bolts or nuts on the suspension components to ensure they're secure.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the recommended type and amount of oil for your vehicle to ensure optimal performance and prevent engine damage.
* Never mix different types of brake pads or rotors, as this can cause uneven braking and potentially lead to accidents.
* Always inspect the suspension components regularly to ensure they're in good condition and replace them as needed to prevent costly repairs.
