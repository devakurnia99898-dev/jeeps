## Step-by-Step Guide
### Introduction to Electric Vehicle Maintenance
Electric vehicles require regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance and longevity. In this section, we will provide a step-by-step guide on how to maintain your Mercedes-AMG electric SUV.

#### Checking the Battery Level
1. Locate the battery level indicator on the dashboard.
2. Check the battery level regularly to ensure it's within the recommended range.
3. Avoid deep discharging the battery, as this can reduce its lifespan.

#### Updating the Software
1. Connect the vehicle to a Wi-Fi network.
2. Check for software updates on the vehicle's infotainment system.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to update the software.

#### Inspecting the Charging System
1. Check the charging cable for any signs of damage or wear.
2. Inspect the charging station for any malfunctioning components.
3. Ensure the charging system is functioning correctly.

#### Rotating the Tires
1. Locate the tire rotation schedule in the owner's manual.
2. Rotate the tires according to the recommended schedule.
3. Check the tire pressure regularly to ensure it's within the recommended range.
