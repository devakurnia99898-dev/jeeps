## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation and Planning
The first step in preparing your garage for the 2027 Dodge Durango is to assess your current tools and equipment. Consider investing in a torque wrench, air compressor, and impact gun to ensure you have the necessary tools for maintenance and repair work.

#### Assessing Your Garage Space
Evaluate your garage space to determine if you have enough room to work on your vehicle comfortably. Consider investing in a lift or jack stands to provide better access to the underside of the vehicle.

#### Researching Maintenance and Repair Procedures
Research the maintenance and repair procedures for the 2027 Dodge Durango, including oil changes, brake pad replacement, and suspension upgrades. Understand the recommended schedules and procedures to ensure you're prepared for any task.

### Oil Change and Fluid Maintenance
Regular oil changes are essential for maintaining the health and performance of your engine. Use the following steps to perform an oil change on your 2027 Dodge Durango:

#### Draining the Oil
Locate the oil drain plug and position a drain pan underneath. Use a 10mm socket to remove the plug and allow the oil to drain completely.

#### Replacing the Oil Filter
Use a filter wrench to remove the oil filter. Dispose of the old filter responsibly and install a new one.

#### Refilling the Oil
Use a funnel to pour in the new oil. Refer to your owner's manual for the recommended oil type and capacity.

### Brake Pad Replacement
Brake pad replacement is a critical maintenance task that requires attention to detail and the right tools. Use the following steps to replace the brake pads on your 2027 Dodge Durango:

#### Removing the Wheels
Use a lug wrench to remove the wheels and gain access to the brake calipers.

#### Removing the Caliper
Use a 10mm socket to remove the caliper bolts. Slide the caliper off the rotor and use a wire to suspend it from the suspension component.

#### Removing the Old Brake Pads
Use pliers to remove the old brake pads from the caliper.

#### Installing the New Brake Pads
Install the new brake pads and reassemble the caliper.

### Suspension Upgrade
A suspension upgrade can significantly improve the performance and handling of your 2027 Dodge Durango. Use the following steps to upgrade your suspension:

#### Removing the Old Suspension Components
Use a 10mm socket to remove the old suspension components, including the shocks and springs.

#### Installing the New Suspension Components
Install the new suspension components, ensuring they are properly aligned and secured.
