## Technical Deep Dive
The Mercedes G-Class 500 GE V8 AMG features a 5.0L V8 engine, producing 240 horsepower and 265 lb-ft of torque. The engine is paired with a 4-speed automatic transmission and a two-speed transfer case, providing power to all four wheels. The suspension system consists of a coil spring front axle and a rear axle with a live axle and coil springs. Common failure points in this system include the coil springs, shock absorbers, and steering components.
