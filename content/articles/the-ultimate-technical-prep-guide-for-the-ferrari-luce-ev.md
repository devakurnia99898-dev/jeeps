## Step-by-Step Guide
### Pre-Maintenance Checks
#### Vehicle Preparation
1. Ensure the vehicle is on a level surface and apply the parking brake.
2. Turn off all accessories and systems to minimize power drain.

#### Diagnostic Scan
1. Connect the diagnostic computer to the vehicle's OBD port.
2. Run a full system scan to identify any existing issues or error codes.

### Battery Maintenance
#### Battery Pack Inspection
1. Locate the battery pack, typically found in the floor pan of the vehicle.
2. Inspect the pack for any signs of damage or leakage.

#### Battery Balancing
1. Use specialized software to access the battery management system.
2. Perform a battery balancing procedure to ensure all cells are at the same state of charge.

### Electric Motor Maintenance
#### Motor Inspection
1. Inspect the electric motors for any signs of wear or overheating.
2. Check the motor bearings for smooth operation.

#### Motor Software Update
1. Connect to the vehicle's software update portal.
2. Download and install the latest software updates for the electric motors.

### Performance Optimization
#### Suspension and Alignment Check
1. Inspect the suspension components for wear.
2. Perform a four-wheel alignment to ensure proper tracking and handling.

#### Tire Pressure and Condition
1. Check the tire pressure and adjust as necessary.
2. Inspect the tires for any signs of uneven wear or damage.
