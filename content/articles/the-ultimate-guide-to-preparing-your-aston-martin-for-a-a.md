## Technical Deep Dive
The Aston Martin DB12 S is a high-performance vehicle that requires regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance. ## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Oil Change
#### Step 1.1: Warm up the engine
Drive your Aston Martin for a few minutes to warm up the engine. This will help the oil drain more easily.
#### Step 1.2: Locate the oil drain plug
Find the oil drain plug located at the bottom of the engine. Use a 10mm socket to remove the plug.
#### Step 1.3: Drain the oil
Allow the oil to drain completely into a pan. Replace the oil drain plug and tighten it to the specified torque.
### Step 2: Brake Pad Replacement
#### Step 2.1: Remove the wheel
Remove the wheel to access the brake caliper. Use a torque wrench to loosen the lug nuts.
#### Step 2.2: Remove the caliper
Remove the caliper to access the brake pads. Use a 10mm socket to remove the caliper bolts.
#### Step 2.3: Install new brake pads
Install the new brake pads and replace the caliper. Tighten the caliper bolts to the specified torque.
### Step 3: Suspension Inspection
#### Step 3.1: Inspect the shock absorbers
Inspect the shock absorbers for any signs of damage or wear. Check the bushings and mounts for any signs of corrosion.
#### Step 3.2: Check the suspension alignment
Check the suspension alignment to ensure it's within the manufacturer's specifications.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the correct type of oil for your Aston Martin. Using the wrong type of oil can damage the engine.
* Don't overtighten the brake caliper bolts. This can damage the brake rotor and cause uneven braking.
* Use a torque wrench to tighten the suspension components. This will ensure the correct torque is applied.

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. **What type of oil should I use for my Aston Martin?**
The recommended oil type for your Aston Martin can be found in the owner's manual. It's essential to use the correct type of oil to ensure optimal engine performance.
2. **How often should I replace my brake pads?**
Brake pads should be replaced every 30,000 to 50,000 miles, depending on driving conditions.
3. **Can I inspect my suspension myself?**
Yes, you can inspect your suspension yourself using a suspension inspection kit. However, if you're not comfortable with the process, it's recommended to consult a professional mechanic.
4. **What are the common signs of worn-out brake pads?**
Common signs of worn-out brake pads include squealing or grinding noises, vibration when braking, and reduced braking performance.
5. **How often should I have my Aston Martin serviced?**
It's recommended to have your Aston Martin serviced every 5,000 to 7,500 miles, depending on driving conditions. Regular maintenance will help ensure your vehicle performs at its best and prevent costly repairs down the road.
