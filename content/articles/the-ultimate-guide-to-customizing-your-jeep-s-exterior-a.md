## **Step-by-Step Guide**
So, how do you go about customizing your Jeep's exterior? Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started:
### **Choose a Color Scheme**
The first step is to choose a color scheme that reflects your desired aesthetic. Consider the color of your vehicle's trim, wheels, and other exterior components, and choose colors that complement them.
#### **Research Different Color Options**
Take some time to research different color options and find inspiration for your project. Look at other Jeeps, as well as other vehicles, and take note of the color schemes that you like.
#### **Consider the 60-30-10 Rule**
The 60-30-10 rule states that 60% of your vehicle's exterior should be a dominant color, 30% a secondary color, and 10% an accent color. This can help you create a balanced, visually appealing color scheme.

### **Prepare the Vehicle's Surface**
Once you've chosen a color scheme, it's time to prepare your vehicle's surface for painting. This involves cleaning the vehicle thoroughly, as well as sanding and polishing the paint to create a smooth, even finish.
#### **Clean the Vehicle**
Start by cleaning the vehicle thoroughly, using a mild detergent and a soft-bristled brush to remove any dirt or debris.
#### **Sand and Polish the Paint**
Next, use a selection of sandpaper and polishing compounds to sand and polish the paint. This will help create a smooth, even finish that's ready for painting.

### **Apply a Two-Tone Design**
If you've decided on a two-tone design, now's the time to apply it. This involves masking off the areas you don't want to paint, and then applying the paint using a paint gun and compressor.
#### **Mask Off the Areas You Don't Want to Paint**
Use a combination of tape and paper to mask off the areas you don't want to paint. Make sure the edges are smooth and even, and that the tape is applied carefully to avoid any wrinkles or bubbles.
#### **Apply the Paint**
Once the areas are masked off, use a paint gun and compressor to apply the paint. Make sure to follow the manufacturer's instructions, and apply the paint in thin, even coats.
