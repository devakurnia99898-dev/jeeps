## Cost Analysis: DIY vs Mechanic
The cost of maintaining and repairing your 2027 Dodge Durango can vary depending on the task and the tools and materials required. However, in general, DIY maintenance and repairs can be more cost-effective than taking your vehicle to a mechanic.

For example, an oil change can cost between $50 and $100 if you do it yourself, compared to $100 to $200 if you take it to a mechanic. Similarly, a tire rotation can cost between $20 and $50 if you do it yourself, compared to $50 to $100 if you take it to a mechanic.
