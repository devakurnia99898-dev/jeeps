## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation
The first step in preparing the 2026 Jeep Wrangler Whitecap for off-road use is to inspect the vehicle's suspension and steering system. This includes checking the ball joints, tie rods, and control arms for any signs of wear or damage.

#### Inspecting the Suspension
1. Raise the vehicle on a lift or jack stands to access the suspension components
2. Inspect the ball joints for any signs of wear or damage, such as loose or worn-out boots
3. Check the tie rods for any signs of wear or damage, such as loose or worn-out ends
4. Inspect the control arms for any signs of wear or damage, such as loose or worn-out bushings

#### Inspecting the Steering System
1. Check the steering gear box for any signs of leaks or damage
2. Inspect the steering column for any signs of wear or damage, such as loose or worn-out bearings
3. Check the power steering pump for any signs of leaks or damage

### Maintenance
The next step is to perform routine maintenance tasks, such as oil changes and tire rotations, to ensure the vehicle is running at optimal levels.

#### Oil Change
1. Locate the oil drain plug and position a drain pan underneath
2. Remove the oil drain plug using a 10mm socket
3. Allow the oil to drain completely before replacing the plug
4. Refill the oil with the recommended type and amount

#### Tire Rotation
1. Raise the vehicle on a lift or jack stands to access the tires
2. Remove the tires and inspect them for any signs of wear or damage
3. Rotate the tires according to the recommended pattern
4. Reinstall the tires and tighten the lug nuts in a star pattern

### Off-Road Preparation
The final step is to prepare the vehicle for off-road use, including installing skid plates and a lift kit.

#### Installing Skid Plates
1. Raise the vehicle on a lift or jack stands to access the underside
2. Remove any existing skid plates or protective covers
3. Install the new skid plates, ensuring they are securely fastened using the recommended hardware

#### Installing a Lift Kit
1. Raise the vehicle on a lift or jack stands to access the suspension
2. Remove the existing suspension components, such as the coil springs and shocks
3. Install the new lift kit components, ensuring they are securely fastened using the recommended hardware
