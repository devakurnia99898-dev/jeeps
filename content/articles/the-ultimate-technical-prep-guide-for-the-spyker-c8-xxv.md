Common failure points on high-performance vehicles like the C8 Preliator XXV include the engine's cooling system, the transmission, and the brakes. ## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation
Before starting any work on the C8 Preliator XXV, ensure the vehicle is parked on a level surface and the parking brake is engaged. Put the transmission in neutral and apply the handbrake.

#### Locating the Engine Bay
The engine bay is located at the front of the vehicle, and can be accessed by releasing the hood latch and lifting the hood.

#### Identifying the Oil Drain Plug
The oil drain plug is located at the bottom of the engine, near the front of the vehicle. It's essential to locate this plug before starting any work, as it will need to be removed to drain the oil.

### Oil Change
#### Draining the Oil
To drain the oil, locate the oil drain plug and remove it using a 10mm socket. Allow the oil to drain completely before replacing the plug.

#### Replacing the Oil Filter
The oil filter is located on top of the engine, and can be removed by hand. Dispose of the old filter responsibly, and install a new one by hand tightening it in a clockwise direction.

#### Refilling the Oil
Refill the oil using the recommended type and amount, as specified in the owner's manual. Replace the oil cap and start the engine, allowing it to run for a few minutes to circulate the new oil.

### Brake Pad Replacement
#### Locating the Brake Calipers
The brake calipers are located on the wheels, and can be accessed by removing the wheel and caliper.

#### Removing the Old Brake Pads
The old brake pads can be removed by releasing the caliper bolts and sliding the caliper off the rotor. Remove the old pads and dispose of them responsibly.

#### Installing the New Brake Pads
The new brake pads can be installed by sliding the caliper back over the rotor and replacing the caliper bolts. Tighten the bolts in a star pattern to ensure even pressure.

### Tire Rotation
#### Locating the Tire Pressure Monitoring System
The tire pressure monitoring system is located on the wheels, and can be accessed by removing the valve stem cap.

#### Rotating the Tires
Rotate the tires according to the recommended pattern, as specified in the owner's manual. Ensure the tire pressure is set to the recommended level, as specified on the tire information placard.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always refer to the owner's manual for specific instructions and recommendations.
* Use the correct type and amount of oil, as specified in the owner's manual.
* Never over-tighten the oil drain plug or caliper bolts, as this can cause damage to the engine or brake system.
