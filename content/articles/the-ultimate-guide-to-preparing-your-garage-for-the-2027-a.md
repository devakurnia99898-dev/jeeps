### Safety Warnings
When working with the 2027 Durango's powertrain, it's essential to take necessary safety precautions. Ensure the vehicle is on a level surface, apply the parking brake, and engage the transmission in park. Wear protective gear, including gloves and safety glasses, when working with tools and electrical systems.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation and Inspection
#### Step 1: Familiarize Yourself with the Vehicle's Systems
Before starting any work, take time to review the 2027 Durango's manual and familiarize yourself with its systems, including the powertrain, suspension, and electrical components.

#### Step 2: Inspect the Vehicle's Condition
Perform a thorough inspection of the vehicle, checking for any signs of wear or damage. Pay particular attention to the engine, transmission, and suspension components.

### Maintenance Essentials
#### Step 3: Oil and Fluid Changes
Regular oil and fluid changes are crucial for the 2027 Durango's maintenance. Use the recommended oil type and viscosity, and ensure the transmission and differential fluids are at the correct levels.

#### Step 4: Brake Pad Replacement
The 2027 Durango's brake pads should be inspected and replaced as needed. Use the correct torque specs when tightening the brake caliper bolts (30 ft-lbs).

#### Step 5: Suspension and Steering System Check
Inspect the suspension and steering system, checking for any signs of wear or damage. Ensure the tire pressure is at the recommended level, and the wheels are properly aligned.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes

* Be cautious when working with the supercharged SRT HELLCAT Jailbreak's complex systems, as incorrect repairs can result in damage or injury.
* Use the correct tools and follow the recommended torque specs to avoid stripping threads or damaging components.

## Cost Analysis: DIY vs Mechanic
The cost of maintaining the 2027 Durango can vary depending on the individual's level of experience and the complexity of the repairs. DIY enthusiasts can save money by performing routine maintenance tasks, such as oil changes and brake pad replacements. However, more complex repairs, such as engine or transmission work, may require the expertise of a professional mechanic.
