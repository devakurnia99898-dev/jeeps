## Technical Deep Dive
The 1970 Dodge Charger R/T Six Pack features a 440 cubic-inch V8 engine, which produces 390 horsepower and 490 lb-ft of torque. The engine is paired with a four-speed manual transmission and features a number of distinctive components, including a Carter AFB carburetor and a dual-exhaust system. The suspension features a coil-spring front end and a leaf-spring rear end, while the electrical system includes a number of vintage components, such as a points-based ignition system.

Common failure points on the 1970 Dodge Charger R/T Six Pack include the water pump, alternator, and transmission seals. The water pump is prone to failure due to its age and the high-performance nature of the engine, while the alternator can fail due to wear and tear on the electrical system. Transmission seals can also fail over time, causing leaks and other issues.

## Step-by-Step Guide
Restoring and maintaining a 1970 Dodge Charger R/T Six Pack requires a thorough and methodical approach. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started:

### Engine Overhaul
The engine is the heart of any classic car, and the 1970 Dodge Charger R/T Six Pack is no exception. To overhaul the engine, follow these steps:

#### Step 1: Remove the Engine
Remove the engine from the car and place it on a engine stand. This will give you access to the engine components and allow you to inspect and repair any damage.

#### Step 2: Disassemble the Engine
Disassemble the engine, removing the cylinder heads, intake manifold, and other components. This will allow you to inspect and repair any damage to the engine internals.

#### Step 3: Inspect and Repair the Engine Internals
Inspect the engine internals, including the pistons, rings, and crankshaft. Repair or replace any damaged components as needed.

#### Step 4: Reassemble the Engine
Reassemble the engine, installing the cylinder heads, intake manifold, and other components. Make sure to torque all bolts and screws to the specified torque specs.

### Transmission Overhaul
The transmission is another critical component of the 1970 Dodge Charger R/T Six Pack. To overhaul the transmission, follow these steps:

#### Step 1: Remove the Transmission
Remove the transmission from the car and place it on a transmission stand. This will give you access to the transmission components and allow you to inspect and repair any damage.

#### Step 2: Disassemble the Transmission
Disassemble the transmission, removing the gearsets, bearings, and other components. This will allow you to inspect and repair any damage to the transmission internals.

#### Step 3: Inspect and Repair the Transmission Internals
Inspect the transmission internals, including the gearsets, bearings, and seals. Repair or replace any damaged components as needed.

#### Step 4: Reassemble the Transmission
Reassemble the transmission, installing the gearsets, bearings, and other components. Make sure to torque all bolts and screws to the specified torque specs.
