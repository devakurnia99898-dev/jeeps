## Step-by-Step Guide
### Pre-Delivery Inspection
#### Checking the Vehicle's History
Check the vehicle's history report to ensure it has no major accidents or damage.

#### Inspecting the Vehicle's Condition
Inspect the vehicle's condition, including the exterior and interior, to ensure it is in good condition.

### Routine Maintenance
#### Oil Change
1. Locate the oil drain plug and position a drain pan underneath.
2. Remove the oil drain plug and allow the oil to drain.
3. Replace the oil filter and add new oil to the recommended level.
4. Tighten the oil drain plug to the specified torque (30 ft-lbs).

#### Tire Rotation
1. Locate the tire rotation pattern in the owner's manual.
2. Loosen the lug nuts on the first wheel using a lug wrench.
3. Raise the vehicle using a jack and remove the lug nuts.
4. Rotate the tire to the next position and hand tighten the lug nuts.
5. Lower the vehicle and tighten the lug nuts to the specified torque (100 ft-lbs).

### Advanced Maintenance
#### Suspension System Inspection
1. Locate the suspension system components, including the coil springs and shock absorbers.
2. Inspect the components for signs of wear or damage.
3. Check the ride height and ensure it is within the recommended specifications.
4. Test the suspension system by driving the vehicle over a rough road surface.

#### Electrical System Inspection
1. Locate the electrical system components, including the battery and wiring.
2. Inspect the components for signs of wear or damage.
3. Check the battery voltage and ensure it is within the recommended specifications.
4. Test the electrical system by turning on the headlights and accessories.

The cost of maintaining the Genesis pickup truck will depend on the specific tasks and the owner's level of expertise.
