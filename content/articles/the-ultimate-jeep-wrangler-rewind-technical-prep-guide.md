## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation
The first step in preparing your Wrangler Rewind for maintenance is to gather all necessary tools and equipment. This includes a 10mm socket, torque wrench, pliers and punch, multimeter, and scan tool.

#### Checking the Oil and Fluids
The next step is to check the oil and fluids in the vehicle. This includes the engine oil, transmission fluid, brake fluid, and coolant. Make sure to check the levels and condition of each fluid, and top them off as needed.

#### Inspecting the Suspension and Steering
The suspension and steering system should also be inspected regularly. Check the condition of the bushings, ball joints, and control arms, and replace them as needed. Also, check the steering gear and pump for any signs of wear or damage.

#### Checking the Brakes
The brake system should also be inspected regularly. Check the condition of the brake pads, rotors, and calipers, and replace them as needed. Also, check the brake fluid level and condition, and top it off as needed.

### Maintenance and Repairs
The next step is to perform any necessary maintenance and repairs. This includes changing the oil and filter, replacing the air filter, and inspecting and replacing the spark plugs.

#### Replacing the Water Pump
If the water pump is failing, it will need to be replaced. This involves draining the coolant, removing the water pump, and installing a new one. Make sure to follow proper torque specs and procedures to avoid damaging the engine or surrounding components.

#### Replacing the Rear Axle Seals
If the rear axle seals are leaking, they will need to be replaced. This involves removing the rear axle, cleaning and inspecting the area, and installing new seals. Make sure to follow proper torque specs and procedures to avoid damaging the axle or surrounding components.

### Final Inspection
The final step is to perform a final inspection of the vehicle. This includes checking all systems, including the engine, transmission, suspension, steering, and brakes. Make sure to test drive the vehicle to ensure it is running properly and safely.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
