## Step-by-Step Guide
In this section, we'll provide a step-by-step guide to performing common maintenance and repair tasks on your Volkswagen pickup truck.

### Oil Change
#### Step 1: Gather materials
Gather the necessary materials, including oil filter wrench, new oil filter, oil drain pan, and enough motor oil to fill the engine.

#### Step 2: Warm up the engine
Warm up the engine by driving the vehicle for a few minutes.

#### Step 3: Find a safe place to drain the oil
Find a level surface and apply the parking brake.

#### Step 4: Drain the oil
Drain the oil into a pan using a socket wrench to remove the oil drain plug.

### Tire Rotation
#### Step 1: Gather materials
Gather the necessary materials, including a lug wrench, tire pressure gauge, and a car jack.

#### Step 2: Loosen the lug nuts
Loosen the lug nuts on the first wheel using a lug wrench.

#### Step 3: Raise the vehicle
Raise the vehicle using a car jack and remove the lug nuts.

#### Step 4: Rotate the tire
Rotate the tire to the next position and hand tighten the lug nuts.
