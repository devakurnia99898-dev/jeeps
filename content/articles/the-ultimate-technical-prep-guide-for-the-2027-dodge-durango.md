## Technical Deep Dive
The 2027 Dodge Durango features a range of advanced systems, including a powerful engine, sophisticated suspension, and cutting-edge electrical components. The engine, for example, is a 3.6L V6 or 5.7L V8, both of which require regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance. Common failure points in the engine include the water pump, timing chain, and oil pump.

The suspension system is another critical component, featuring a coil-over shock absorber design. This system provides a smooth ride and excellent handling, but can be prone to wear and tear over time. Common failure points in the suspension include the coil springs, shock absorbers, and control arms.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation
Before starting any work, ensure you have a clean and well-lit workspace. Gather all the necessary tools and materials, and consult your owner's manual for specific instructions.

#### Checking the Oil Level
1. Locate the oil dipstick under the hood.
2. Pull out the dipstick and wipe it clean with a rag.
3. Insert the dipstick and pull it out again to check the oil level.
4. Add oil as needed to reach the recommended level.

#### Rotating the Tires
1. Locate the spare tire and tools.
2. Loosen the lug nuts on the first wheel using a torque wrench.
3. Raise the vehicle using a jack and remove the lug nuts.
4. Remove the wheel and replace it with the spare tire.
5. Tighten the lug nuts in a star pattern to the recommended torque spec (100 ft-lbs).

#### Inspecting the Suspension
1. Locate the coil springs and shock absorbers.
2. Inspect the coil springs for signs of wear or damage.
3. Check the shock absorbers for leaks or damage.
4. Inspect the control arms for signs of wear or damage.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always refer to your owner's manual for specific instructions and recommendations.
* Use the correct tools and materials to avoid damaging your vehicle.
* Don't over-tighten bolts or lug nuts, as this can cause damage to the threads.
* Keep your vehicle clean and well-maintained to prevent rust and corrosion.
