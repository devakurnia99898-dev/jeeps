## Technical Deep Dive
A suspension system consists of several key components, including:
* Springs (coil or leaf)
* Shocks (hydraulic or gas-filled)
* Control arms (upper and lower)
* Steering linkage
* Bushings and mounts

Common failure points in a suspension system include:
* Worn-out bushings and mounts
* Damaged or corroded control arms
* Leaking or damaged shocks
* Weak or broken springs

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Inspect the Suspension System
Inspect the suspension system for any signs of wear or damage. Check the bushings and mounts for any signs of cracking or deterioration. Look for any signs of corrosion on the control arms and steering linkage.

#### Step 1.1: Remove the Wheels
Remove the wheels to access the suspension system. Use a 10mm socket to loosen the lug nuts, then raise the vehicle using a lift or jack.

#### Step 1.2: Inspect the Control Arms
Inspect the control arms for any signs of damage or corrosion. Check the bushings and mounts for any signs of wear or deterioration.

### Step 2: Replace the Bushings and Mounts
Replace the bushings and mounts with new ones. Use a bushing press to remove the old bushings and install the new ones.

#### Step 2.1: Remove the Old Bushings
Use a bushing press to remove the old bushings. Apply lubricant to the new bushings and press them into place.

#### Step 2.2: Install the New Mounts
Install the new mounts, ensuring they are securely fastened using the recommended torque specs.

### Step 3: Replace the Shocks
Replace the shocks with new ones. Use a shock absorber tool to remove the old shocks and install the new ones.

#### Step 3.1: Remove the Old Shocks
Use a shock absorber tool to remove the old shocks. Take note of the orientation of the shock absorber to ensure correct installation.

#### Step 3.2: Install the New Shocks
Install the new shocks, ensuring they are securely fastened using the recommended torque specs.

### Step 4: Replace the Springs
Replace the springs with new ones. Use a spring compressor to remove the old springs and install the new ones.

#### Step 4.1: Remove the Old Springs
Use a spring compressor to remove the old springs. Take note of the orientation of the spring to ensure correct installation.

#### Step 4.2: Install the New Springs
Install the new springs, ensuring they are securely fastened using the recommended torque specs.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the recommended torque specs to avoid damaging the suspension components
* Ensure the vehicle is properly aligned after replacing the suspension system
* Don't overtighten the bushings and mounts, as this can cause damage to the suspension system
