## Step-by-Step Guide
### Preparation
#### Step 1: Gather Tools and Materials
Gather all the necessary tools and materials, including the owner's manual, before starting any maintenance or repair work.

#### Step 2: Familiarize Yourself with the Vehicle
Familiarize yourself with the vehicle's systems, including the engine, transmission, suspension, and braking systems.

### Oil Change
#### Step 1: Warm Up the Engine
Warm up the engine by driving the vehicle for a few minutes to circulate the oil.

#### Step 2: Locate the Oil Drain Plug
Locate the oil drain plug, which is usually located at the bottom of the engine.

#### Step 3: Remove the Oil Drain Plug
Remove the oil drain plug using a 10mm socket and allow the oil to drain into a pan.

#### Step 4: Replace the Oil Filter
Replace the oil filter with a new one, making sure to tighten it securely.

#### Step 5: Refill the Oil
Refill the oil with the recommended type and amount, usually 6 quarts of 5W-20 oil.

### Tire Rotation
#### Step 1: Check the Tire Pressure
Check the tire pressure using a tire pressure gauge and adjust it to the recommended level.

#### Step 2: Loosen the Lug Nuts
Loosen the lug nuts using a 12mm socket, but do not remove them yet.

#### Step 3: Raise the Vehicle
Raise the vehicle using a jack and support it with jack stands.

#### Step 4: Remove the Lug Nuts
Remove the lug nuts and rotate the tires according to the recommended pattern.

#### Step 5: Tighten the Lug Nuts
Tighten the lug nuts in a star pattern to ensure even tightening.

### Spark Plug Replacement
#### Step 1: Locate the Spark Plugs
Locate the spark plugs, which are usually located on the side of the engine.

#### Step 2: Remove the Spark Plug Wire
Remove the spark plug wire using a spark plug wire puller.

#### Step 3: Remove the Spark Plug
Remove the spark plug using a spark plug socket and a torque wrench.

#### Step 4: Install the New Spark Plug
Install the new spark plug, making sure to tighten it securely.

#### Step 5: Reinstall the Spark Plug Wire
Reinstall the spark plug wire, making sure it is securely connected.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Always use the recommended type and amount of oil to prevent premature wear and tear.
* Make sure to tighten the lug nuts in a star pattern to ensure even tightening.
* Avoid over-tightening the spark plugs, as this can damage the engine.
