## Technical Deep Dive
The Oceanic Entry Point is a designated point where aircraft transition from domestic airspace to oceanic airspace. This point is typically marked by a specific latitude and longitude coordinate, and pilots must enter this point at a precise time to ensure safe and efficient flight operations.

The system works as follows:
* Pilots receive clearance from air traffic control to enter the oceanic airspace
* They must then navigate to the designated Oceanic Entry Point using a combination of GPS, inertial navigation, and celestial navigation
* Once at the entry point, pilots must establish communication with oceanic air traffic control and report their position, altitude, and heading
* They must also comply with specific procedures for navigation, communication, and emergency protocols
