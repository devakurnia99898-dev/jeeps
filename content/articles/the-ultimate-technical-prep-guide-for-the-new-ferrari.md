## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Pre-Maintenance Inspection
#### Step 1.1: Visual Inspection
Inspect the vehicle's exterior and interior for any signs of damage or wear. Check the tires, brakes, and suspension for any signs of excessive wear.

#### Step 1.2: Fluid Check
Check the engine oil, transmission fluid, and coolant levels. Ensure they are at the recommended levels and that there are no signs of leaks.

#### Step 1.3: Battery Check
Check the battery's condition and charge level. Ensure it is fully charged and that the terminals are clean and secure.

### Step 2: Routine Maintenance
#### Step 2.1: Oil Change
Change the engine oil and filter according to the manufacturer's schedule. Use the recommended type and amount of oil.

#### Step 2.2: Tire Rotation
Rotate the tires according to the manufacturer's schedule. Ensure they are properly inflated and balanced.

#### Step 2.3: Brake Pad Replacement
Replace the brake pads according to the manufacturer's schedule. Ensure they are properly installed and that the brake fluid is at the recommended level.

### Step 3: Advanced Maintenance
#### Step 3.1: Timing Chain Replacement
Replace the timing chain according to the manufacturer's schedule. Ensure it is properly installed and that the engine is properly timed.

#### Step 3.2: Transmission Service
Service the transmission according to the manufacturer's schedule. Ensure the fluid is at the recommended level and that the filter is clean.

#### Step 3.3: Suspension Service
Service the suspension according to the manufacturer's schedule. Ensure the adaptive damping system is properly calibrated and that the ride height is set to the recommended level.
