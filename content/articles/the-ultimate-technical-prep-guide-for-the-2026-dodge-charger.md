## Technical Deep Dive
The 2026 Dodge Charger PPV is powered by a 3.6L V6 engine, producing 292 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. The engine is paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and features a rear-wheel-drive layout. The suspension system is specially designed to handle the rigors of police work, with a heavy-duty coil-over shock absorber and a stabilizer bar. Common failure points in the 2026 Dodge Charger PPV include the water pump, alternator, and brake pads. The electrical system is also a critical component, with a complex network of wiring and sensors that require regular maintenance.

## Step-by-Step Guide
### Pre-Maintenance Checks
#### Visual Inspection
Inspect the vehicle for any signs of damage or wear, including dents, scratches, and rust spots. Check the tire pressure and tread depth, and ensure the spare tire is in good condition.
#### Fluid Checks
Check the engine oil, transmission fluid, brake fluid, and coolant levels. Top off any fluids that are low, and replace the engine oil and filter as recommended by the manufacturer.
### Maintenance Tasks
#### Oil Change
1. Locate the oil drain plug and position a drip pan underneath
2. Remove the oil drain plug and allow the oil to drain completely
3. Replace the oil filter and refill the engine with the recommended type and amount of oil
4. Tighten the oil drain plug to the specified torque (30 ft-lbs)
#### Brake Pad Replacement
1. Raise the vehicle and remove the wheels
2. Remove the caliper and rotor
3. Inspect the brake pads and replace them if necessary
4. Reinstall the caliper and rotor, and tighten the caliper bolts to the specified torque (20 ft-lbs)
#### Water Pump Replacement
1. Locate the water pump and disconnect the electrical connector
2. Remove the serpentine belt and water pump pulley
3. Remove the water pump mounting bolts and pull the pump out of the engine compartment
4. Install a new water pump and tighten the mounting bolts to the specified torque (30 ft-lbs)
