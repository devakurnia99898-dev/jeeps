As Ferrari ventures into the world of electric and biofuels, it's essential for owners and enthusiasts to understand the technical implications of these new technologies. ## Technical Deep Dive
Biofuels and electric systems require a deep understanding of the underlying technologies. Biofuels, such as biocarburants, are derived from organic matter and can be used to power internal combustion engines. However, they require specific engine modifications to ensure compatibility and optimal performance. Electric systems, on the other hand, rely on advanced battery technology and electric motors to propel the vehicle. Common failure points in electric systems include the battery management system, electric motor, and power electronics.
