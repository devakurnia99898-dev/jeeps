## Step-by-Step Guide
### Step 1: Engine Preparation
#### Step 1.1: Upgrade the Air Intake System
Install a high-performance air intake system, such as a cold air intake or a ram air intake, to increase engine power and efficiency. Ensure the intake system is properly sealed and secured to prevent air leaks.
#### Step 1.2: Upgrade the Exhaust System
Install a high-performance exhaust system, such as a cat-back exhaust or a header-back exhaust, to increase engine power and reduce backpressure. Ensure the exhaust system is properly installed and secured to prevent leaks or damage.
### Step 2: Suspension Preparation
#### Step 2.1: Upgrade the Suspension Components
Install adjustable coilovers and sway bars to improve handling and stability. Ensure the suspension components are properly installed and aligned to prevent uneven wear or damage.
#### Step 2.2: Align the Suspension
Use suspension alignment tools to ensure the vehicle's suspension is properly aligned and adjusted. This will help improve handling and reduce the risk of uneven wear or damage.
### Step 3: Braking System Preparation
#### Step 3.1: Upgrade the Brake Pads
Install high-performance brake pads, such as ceramic or carbon fiber brake pads, to improve braking performance and reduce wear. Ensure the brake pads are properly installed and secured to prevent damage or failure.
#### Step 3.2: Upgrade the Brake Fluid
Install high-performance brake fluid, such as synthetic or racing brake fluid, to improve braking performance and reduce wear. Ensure the brake fluid is properly installed and secured to prevent damage or failure.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
* Don't neglect to properly secure and support the vehicle before starting work.
* Don't overlook the importance of proper suspension alignment and adjustment.
* Don't use low-quality or inferior components, as they can compromise performance and safety.
* Tricks of the trade include using a torque wrench to ensure proper tightening and using a welder to secure components.
