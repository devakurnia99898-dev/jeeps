## Step-by-Step Guide
### Introduction to the GV90's Systems
The GV90 features a range of complex systems, including its engine, transmission, and all-wheel-drive system. Understanding how these systems work is crucial for maintenance and repair.

#### Engine Overview
The GV90's engine is a 3.5-liter twin-turbo V6, producing 375 horsepower and 391 lb-ft of torque. This engine features a range of advanced technologies, including direct fuel injection and variable valve timing.

#### Transmission and All-Wheel-Drive System
The GV90's transmission is an 8-speed automatic, featuring a range of advanced technologies, including paddle shifters and a manual mode. The all-wheel-drive system features an advanced torque vectoring system, allowing for precise control and stability.

### Maintenance Schedule
The GV90 requires regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance and longevity. This includes regular oil changes, tire rotations, and brake pad replacements.

#### Oil Change Procedure
1. Locate the oil drain plug and position the oil drain pan underneath.
2. Remove the oil drain plug using a 10mm socket.
3. Allow the oil to drain completely before replacing the oil drain plug.
4. Refill the engine with the recommended oil type and amount.

#### Brake Pad Replacement Procedure
1. Remove the wheel using a lug wrench.
2. Remove the caliper using a 10mm socket.
3. Remove the old brake pad and replace with a new one.
4. Reinstall the caliper and wheel.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues
The GV90 can experience a range of issues, including problems with the infotainment system and adaptive suspension. Understanding how to troubleshoot these issues is crucial for maintaining the vehicle.

#### Infotainment System Troubleshooting
1. Check the infotainment system's software for updates.
2. Restart the infotainment system.
3. Check the connections to the infotainment system.

#### Adaptive Suspension Troubleshooting
1. Check the adaptive dampers for any signs of wear or damage.
2. Check the suspension's software for updates.
3. Check the connections to the adaptive dampers.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
When working on the GV90, it's essential to avoid common mistakes, such as over-tightening bolts or using the wrong type of oil. Pro tips include using a torque wrench to ensure accurate torque specs and consulting the owner's manual for specific instructions.
