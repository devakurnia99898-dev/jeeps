## Step-by-Step Guide
### Pre-Delivery Inspection
Before taking delivery of the 2027 Dodge Durango, it's essential to conduct a thorough inspection to ensure the vehicle is in good condition. This includes checking the exterior and interior for any damage, testing the engine and suspension system, and reviewing the vehicle's history.

#### Checking the Exterior
* Inspect the paint job for any scratches or damage
* Check the tires for proper inflation and tread depth
* Test the lights and signals to ensure they are functioning correctly

#### Checking the Interior
* Inspect the seats and upholstery for any damage
* Test the infotainment system and ensure all features are functioning correctly
* Check the dashboard and instrument cluster for any warning lights or issues

#### Testing the Engine and Suspension
* Take the vehicle for a test drive to ensure the engine and suspension system are functioning smoothly
* Check for any unusual noises or vibrations
* Test the brakes to ensure they are functioning correctly

### Routine Maintenance
To keep the 2027 Dodge Durango running smoothly, it's essential to follow a routine maintenance schedule. This includes regular oil changes, tire rotations, and brake pad replacements.

#### Oil Change
* Locate the oil drain plug and position a drain pan underneath
* Remove the oil drain plug and allow the oil to drain
* Replace the oil filter and add new oil
* Tighten the oil drain plug to the specified torque (30 ft-lbs)

#### Tire Rotation
* Locate the tire rotation pattern in the owner's manual
* Raise the vehicle on a lift and remove the wheels
* Rotate the tires according to the specified pattern
* Tighten the lug nuts to the specified torque (100 ft-lbs)

#### Brake Pad Replacement
* Locate the brake caliper and remove the caliper bolts
* Remove the old brake pads and inspect the rotors for any damage
* Install the new brake pads and tighten the caliper bolts to the specified torque (25 ft-lbs)

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
When working on the 2027 Dodge Durango, it's essential to avoid common mistakes that can lead to costly repairs. One common mistake is over-tightening the oil drain plug, which can damage the threads. Another mistake is not following the specified torque pattern for the lug nuts, which can lead to uneven tire wear.

To avoid these mistakes, it's essential to follow the manufacturer's instructions and take necessary precautions. Always use a torque wrench to ensure the correct torque is applied, and never over-tighten any bolts or nuts.
