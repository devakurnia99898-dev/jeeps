## Step-by-Step Guide
### Prepare Your Garage
#### Clear the Workspace
Clear your garage of any clutter or obstacles to create a safe and efficient workspace. Remove any flammable materials and ensure that the floor is level and even.

#### Install a Lift or Jack
Consider installing a lift or jack to make it easier to work underneath the vehicle. Ensure that the lift or jack is rated for the weight of the Genesis pickup truck.

#### Organize Your Tools
Organize your tools and equipment in a logical and accessible manner. Consider investing in a tool chest or cabinet to keep your tools clean and protected.

### Purchase Necessary Tools
#### Research and Compare Prices
Research and compare prices for the necessary tools and equipment. Consider purchasing a scan tool and multimeter to help diagnose any issues with the vehicle's electrical system.

#### Purchase a Torque Wrench
Purchase a torque wrench that is capable of handling the high torque specs of the Genesis pickup truck's engine and suspension.

#### Consider a Air Compressor
Consider purchasing an air compressor to power your tools and equipment. Ensure that the air compressor is rated for the demands of your tools.

### Inspect the Vehicle
#### Visual Inspection
Perform a visual inspection of the vehicle to identify any damage or wear. Check the tires, brakes, and suspension for any signs of wear or damage.

#### Use a Scan Tool
Use a scan tool to diagnose any issues with the vehicle's electrical system. Check for any trouble codes or alerts that may indicate a problem.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes
